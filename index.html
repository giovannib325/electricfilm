What Is Smart Window Tint?

Smart Window Tint — also known as PDLC (Polymer Dispersed Liquid Crystal) film — is an advanced optoelectronic layer laminated into glass that lets you switch between clear and private instantly with electricity. Unlike traditional tint or frosted glass, it gives you real-time control without curtains, blinds, or permanent coatings.
